A team of agents, run by a supervisor

Not a fixed pipeline — a supervisor that decides who works next and reacts to your instructions. Every worker reports back before the next one starts.

Casts the panel from a library of 100+ personas; hires new ones only for the gaps.

05

A moderator debrief, deterministic stats, and a decision-ready readout with caveats.

Grounded in what real customers say

Name your competitors and the research agent pulls their App Store reviews and forum threads, distils the genuine objections and vocabulary, and feeds them into every persona's reaction — so the output reads like research, not a language model guessing.

A persona library that compounds

Every synthetic person is a file you can open and edit — with a household, the products they use today, a specific recent frustration, and their own way of talking. Studies recruit from the library first and only manufacture the shortfall, so the population grows with every run.
